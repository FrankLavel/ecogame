# Arte de EcoGame 2

Herramienta: generación de imágenes integrada (`image_gen`), sin API externa configurada por el usuario. Originales de alta resolución conservados en esta carpeta; las copias importadas están en `Animations/`.

## Prompts de producción

**park.png — edición de la distribución original.** Refine the supplied 16:9 top-down park map into a premium hand-painted cozy adventure environment. Preserve the exact path geometry, pond centered at (210,275), benches at (1020,300) and (200,535), and empty bin platform x400–880 y145–255. Detailed leaf clusters, dappled warm lighting, grass, flowers, stone edging, roots, reeds, turquoise rippling water and textured wood. Keep walkable areas clear. No characters, bins, trash, UI or lettering.

**duck.png — sprite del jefe, fondo transparente.** Don Mugron, comically menacing giant mallard park-littering villain. Entire body visible from an overhead adventure game viewpoint. Iridescent emerald head, angry expressive eyebrows, orange bill, cream feathered belly, layered detailed wings and orange webbed feet. Crooked crown made from a dented tin can, old tire as belt, paper scraps caught in feathers. Premium hand-painted cartoon game art, crisp silhouette, detailed feathers and warm highlights. Family-friendly, no blood, weapons, text, scenery or watermark.

**ranger.png — atlas transparente de cuatro poses.** Exact 2 × 2 walking sprite sheet of the same friendly ranger, same size and baseline: idle, left-foot step, passing pose, right-foot step. Tan skin, brown eyes, green wide-brim hat with golden leaf badge, sage jacket, yellow shirt, small backpack, navy trousers, brown hiking boots and gloves. Slightly overhead camera, detailed painterly material texture and consistent light. No grid, labels, scenery or watermark.

**bins.png — atlas transparente de tres contenedores.** Three separate equal-scale bins in one horizontal row with margins: green organic bin with leaf emblem, yellow packaging bin with bottle-and-can emblem, blue paper bin with paper emblem. Sturdy wheeled park bins with hinged lids, slots, handles, bolts and softly weathered material. Detailed hand-painted game style, overhead frontal view, warm light. No letters, words, numbers, grid or scenery. Las etiquetas se añaden al importar para que coincidan con las reglas del juego.

**waste.png — atlas transparente de seis residuos.** Exact 3 × 2 item sheet: banana peel, apple core, crumpled PET bottle; dented aluminum can, clean crumpled paper, opened cardboard box. Items centered in equal cells with transparent gaps. Detailed painterly materials, warm light, subtle shadows, readable silhouettes and slightly overhead view. No brands, text, grids or scenery.

Los atlas se separan por sus celdas y se adaptan al tamaño del juego; los originales no se sobrescriben. El mapa conserva la geometría de las colisiones. La versión anterior se conserva en `archive/v1/Entrega-EcoGame.zip`.
